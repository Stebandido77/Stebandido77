# Hi there, I'm Esteban 👋

> *Derivatives by day, Batman by night — investigating behavior, education, and financial decisions.*

I'm a **financial consultant & applied economist** based in Bogotá, Colombia. I bridge quantitative finance and empirical research: by day I work in capital markets and risk; by night I build tools, write, and investigate how people make decisions under uncertainty.
